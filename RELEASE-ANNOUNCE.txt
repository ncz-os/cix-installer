NCZ-OS 26.10 "Cygnus" is available for CIX Sky1 (arm64) and AMD64 (including Apple T2 Macs).
It ships 74 screensavers and updates the Singularity desktop, greeter, and boot splash.
Both architectures default to the 7.2.9 stable kernel, which is also the installer boot kernel.
Sky1 (arm64) adds a non-default 7.3.0-rc6 edge kernel; AMD64 ships the 7.2.9 default only.
Installer, boot, clock, GPU-health, and Sky1 audio fixes are included.
The release is based on Debian testing (Forky).
Radxa Orion EFI 1.3.1 or newer is required on Sky1 boards.
Not shipped: the AMD64 edge kernel and the full Cygnus visual theme.
Known issue: on T2 Macs the internal keyboard is inactive during the installer; use a USB keyboard.
